🚀 HOW TO BOOST 🚀

1· Get Discord Nitro

  • A Discord Nitro subscription includes 2 Server Boosts.
  • Nitro Basic does not include Server Boosts.

See “HOW TO GET NITRO” below to learn how to subscribe.

2· Open the Server Menu

  • Click on the server name at the top of Discord.
  • Select “Boost This Server.”

3· Use your Server Boosts

  • You can use both of your included boosts to support the server.

4· Confirm your Boosts

  • Follow the instructions to confirm your boosts.
  • Your boosts will be applied to the server immediately.

5· Enjoy your Perks

  • As a server booster, you can request your own custom role in ❯❯ # 🎨︱Custom-Role [💎]
  • Your role will change the color of your name, and an icon can be set next to your name.

💳 HOW TO GET NITRO 💳

Don't have Nitro yet? You can subscribe directly through Discord.

1· Open User Settings

  • Click the ⚙️ User Settings button at the bottom of the channel list.

2· Select “Nitro”

  • Choose the Nitro subscription and follow Discord's instructions.

3· Come back and Boost

  • Once your Nitro subscription is active, you'll receive 2 Server Boosts.
  • Follow the steps above to use them on this server! 🚀
